# Code of Conduct

Project participation must be respectful, professional and free from
harassment. Technical criticism should be evidence-based and directed at the
work, not the person. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, discriminatory,
threatening, unlawful or irrelevant contributions and may restrict repeated
misconduct. Conduct concerns can be reported through the contact information in
the [imprint](https://riskdatascience.net/impressum/).
